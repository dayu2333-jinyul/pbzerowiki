Extreme Difficulty
Endgame challenges requiring maxed gear, perfect parry timing, and full Sha-Chi mastery.
#1

Inverse Heaven

Final Boss · True Ending
⚡ 4 Phases📊 Rec. Level 45+⚔️ Flame Tongue or Phoenix Wing
9.5/10

Four distinct phases: celestial bullet hell, shadow clone army, reality-warping hazards, and a final duel with no healing windows. Phase 3 demands perfect Ghoststep timing through 8 consecutive unblockable attacks. Bring maxed Flame Tongue for bleed burst in Phase 2, or Phoenix Wing for sustained DPS in Phase 4's damage race. → Full guide

⚔️
Hard Difficulty
Major story bosses with complex mechanics. Requires solid parry fundamentals and build optimization.
#2

Seven Judgments

Mid-Game Gatekeeper · Story Boss
⚡ 3 Phases📊 Rec. Level 30⚔️ Jagged Steel or Ironbreaker
8.0/10

The skill check. Seven attack strings with different parry timings. Phase 2 adds a Brutal Move grab combo that one-shots under-leveled players. Phase 3's arena floods with Sha-Chi mist that drains your bar — forces aggressive play. Jagged Steel's bleed buildup ideal for mist phase. → Full guide

#3

Snake Sovereign

Poison Arena · Optional Boss
⚡ 3 Phases📊 Rec. Level 28⚔️ Serpent Fang or Twilight Spear
7.2/10

Poison swamp arena — constant HP drain forces a DPS race. Phase 2 tracking poison orbs while maintaining damage. Phase 3 boss goes underground for ambush attacks. Spear weapons safest; Serpent Fang's toxin resist passive trivializes the poison. → Full guide

🗿
Moderate Difficulty
Early to mid-game encounters. Challenging for new players but learnable.
#4

Ogre Hammer Lord

Early-Game Wall · Required
⚡ 2 Phases📊 Rec. Level 15⚔️ Venomous Softblade or Crescent Moon
5.5/10

The first real test. Telegraphed swings make parry timing easy to learn, but damage is punishing if you miss. Phase 2 enrage timer — if the fight drags, the boss gains +30% damage and speed. Venomous Softblade's extended parry frames make this much more forgiving. → Full guide

Recommended Boss Order (Easiest Path)

  1. Ogre Hammer Lord (Lv 15) — Learn parry fundamentals
  2. Snake Sovereign (Lv 28) — Get toxin-resist gear first
  3. Seven Judgments (Lv 30) — The skill check, expect 10-20 attempts
  4. Inverse Heaven (Lv 45+) — Endgame, maxed gear and 20+ heals

How We Score Bosses

  • Attack Pattern Complexity (30%) — Number of unique attacks, mixups, and tells
  • Damage Output (25%) — How many hits to kill at recommended level
  • Phase Mechanics (25%) — Gimmick difficulty, phase transition hazards
  • Parry/Counter Difficulty (20%) — Tightness of parry windows, unblockable attack count
