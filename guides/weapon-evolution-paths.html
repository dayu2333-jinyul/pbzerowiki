Weapon Evolution Paths

Every weapon can evolve through multiple tiers. This guide covers every upgrade tree, material requirements, and which path fits your build.

Home / Guides / Weapon Evolution Paths

Phantom Blade Zero's weapon evolution system allows each weapon to upgrade through three tiers, with branching paths at the final tier. Unlike traditional upgrade systems where a +X weapon is strictly better, PBZ's branching evolutions let you customize your weapon's stat profile and passive effect. Choosing the right evolution path is as important as choosing the right weapon.

Evolution Basics

Every weapon starts at Tier 1 (base form). Upgrading requires materials, and each tier unlocks new stat bonuses and sometimes new passive abilities. The system works as follows:

TierLevel RangeMaterials NeededStat Bonus per LevelSpecial
Tier 1 (Base)1-15Scrap Metal, Rusted Ore+2 Attack, +1% Sha-Chi GenWeapon passive unlocked at Level 5
Tier 2 (Refined)16-30Refined Ore, Phantom Essence+3 Attack, +2% Sha-Chi GenPhantom Edge slot unlocked at Level 20
Tier 3 (Evolved)31-45Primal Shard, Soul Fragment+5 Attack, +3% Sha-Chi GenBranching passive choice at Level 35
Important: Evolution materials are shared across all weapons, but the cost increases with each weapon you upgrade. Your first weapon to Tier 3 costs 15 Primal Shards. Your second costs 30. Plan which weapons you evolve carefully — you cannot max every weapon in a single playthrough.

Weapon Evolution Trees

Sword Evolution

Jagged Steel

Starting sword with balanced stats and bleed buildup. The most versatile weapon in the game with the most flexible evolution tree.

Tier 2 (Tempered Steel): +15% base damage, +10% Sha-Chi generation rate. Unlocks a new heavy attack chain with improved stagger.

Tier 3A (Serrated Fang): Bleed stack cap increases from 5 to 7. Each bleed tick deals 10% more damage. Recommended for Bleed Build and Aggressive Chi Rush.

Tier 3B (Iron Will): Parry window extends by 2 frames (from 8 to 10 frames). Perfect parry window extends by 1 frame. Recommended for Defensive Parry Master.

Tier 3B (Mirror Blade): Parrying ranged attacks reflects them back at the attacker with 150% damage. Essential for certain boss fights with heavy ranged phases (Snake Sovereign's Venom Spit).

Tier 3B (Phoenix Rebirth): Every fire explosion heals 5% of max HP. Provides excellent sustain for aggressive play. Combined with Blood Sigil, this can keep you at full HP through most group encounters.

Tier 3B (Earthbreaker): Fully charged heavy attacks send out a shockwave that hits enemies in a line. Adds much-needed range to the greatsword's kit. Better for new players who need safe damage options.

General Rule: Invest your first 15 Primal Shards into your primary weapon's Tier 3. A Tier 3 weapon outperforms two Tier 2 weapons in most situations. Only split materials across weapons once your primary is at Tier 3.

Common Mistakes

  • Upgrading too many weapons: Materials scale in cost per weapon. Focus on 2-3 weapons max per playthrough.
  • Ignoring Tier 2 Phantom Edge slots: Every Tier 2 weapon unlocks a second Phantom Edge slot at level 20. This doubles your utility options. Do not switch to a new weapon before reaching level 20.
  • Wrong Tier 3 branch: Read the passive description carefully before choosing. The wrong branch cannot be reset — there is no respec option for weapon evolutions.
  • Over-farming early game materials: Scrap Metal and Rusted Ore are abundant. Do not spend hours farming them — you will naturally accumulate enough through story progression.
