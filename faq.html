Phantom Blade Zero FAQ

what you need to know about the game -- release date, platforms, price, combat system, and more.

Home / FAQ
▶ When does Phantom Blade Zero release?
September 9, 2026 for PlayStation 5 and PC (Steam). Pre-orders are expected to open 3-4 months before release.
▶ What platforms is Phantom Blade Zero on?
PlayStation 5 and PC (Steam). A Nintendo Switch 2 or Xbox Series X|S release has not been announced. Given the game's Unreal Engine 5 requirements, a last-gen (PS4/Xbox One) port is unlikely.
▶ How long is Phantom Blade Zero?
The main story takes approximately 30-40 hours. If you explore all regions, complete side missions, and pursue all 8 endings, expect 60-80+ hours total. New Game Plus and boss rush modes add significant replay value.
▶ Is there multiplayer?
Phantom Blade Zero includes co-op dungeons and a boss rush endgame mode with leaderboards. The main campaign is single-player only. Co-op sessions support up to 3 players in dedicated dungeon instances separate from the story.
▶ Who is the developer?
S-GAME, a studio based in Beijing, China. They previously developed the Phantom Blade mobile franchise. Phantom Blade Zero is their first console/PC title and represents a significant leap in scope and production value.
▶ What engine does it use?
Unreal Engine 5. The game leverages Nanite virtualized geometry for detailed environments and Lumen dynamic global illumination for realistic lighting. This enables the high-fidelity visual style shown in trailers.
▶ Is Phantom Blade Zero open world?
It is semi-open with interconnected regions. Each area is sizable with branching paths, shortcuts, and secrets, but progression is gated by story milestones. Think of it like a modern God of War -- large, explorable zones rather than a seamless open world.
▶ How many endings are there?
8 distinct endings. Your choices throughout the game -- key NPC relationships, faction allegiances, specific boss outcomes, and a critical decision in the final act -- determine which ending you unlock. Multiple playthroughs are required to see them all.
▶ Is it a Souls-like?
Phantom Blade Zero is closer to Ninja Gaiden or Devil May Cry than Dark Souls. The Sha-Chi system rewards aggressive offense -- there is no stamina bar, combat is fast-paced, and you have deep combo strings. It shares some DNA with Souls games (boss-focused, checkpoint system) but plays very differently.
▶ How much will it cost?
The price has not been officially announced. Based on current AAA action game pricing, expect $59.99-$69.99 USD. A deluxe or collector's edition with bonus content (art book, soundtrack, in-game items) is likely. Pre-order details are expected closer to launch.
