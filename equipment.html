Equipment & Items

Everything you can equip in Phantom Blade Zero — armor sets, accessories, consumables, and key items. Optimize your loadout for any challenge.

Home / Equipment

Armor Sets

Armor in Phantom Blade Zero provides defense, elemental resistance, and set bonuses. Wearing multiple pieces from the same set unlocks additional passive effects. Armor does not affect movement speed or dodge timing.

Phantom Blade Garb

Uncommon

The standard attire of Phantom Blade assassins. Balanced defense with +5% attack speed. Found early in the game.

+8 Defense Set (2): +10% Crit Damage

Order Sentinel Armor

Rare

Heavy armor worn by the Order's elite guards. High defense but slightly reduced Sha-Chi generation. Drops from Order Sentinel mini-bosses.

+15 Defense Set (3): +20% Parry Window

Healer's Robes

Uncommon

Light robes worn by the Healer's Circle. Low defense but boosts HP regeneration and Sha-Chi recovery. Obtained via Healer's Circle side quests.

+4 Defense Set (2): +30% Healing

Iron Wall Set

Common

Standard soldier armor offering consistent protection. No special effects but easy to upgrade with basic materials.

+6 Defense Set (2): +5% Damage Reduction

Shadow Stalker Gear

Rare

Lightweight gear designed for speed. Increases movement speed and dodge distance. Found in late-game areas.

+5 Defense Set (3): Ghoststep costs 0 bars

Dragon Lord Armor

Legendary

Legendary armor set hidden in a secret boss arena. Grants fire resistance and a chance to ignite attackers. Fully upgraded defense rivals any set.

+18 Defense Set (3): Flame Aura (5 fire damage/sec to nearby enemies)

Accessories

Accessories provide passive stat bonuses and unique effects. You can equip up to 3 accessories at a time.

Jade Pendant

Common

Increases max HP by 10%. Found in the starting area.

+10% Max HP

Sha-Chi Charm

Uncommon

Reduces Sha-Chi decay delay by 1 second. Useful for all builds.

Decay Delay +1s

Blood Amber Ring

Rare

Lifesteal: heal 3% of damage dealt. Drops from Snake Sovereign boss.

3% Lifesteal

Windwalker Boots

Uncommon

Increases dodge distance by 15%. Found in the first open-world region.

+15% Dodge Distance

Order Seal

Rare

Boosts damage against Order enemies by 20%. Drops from the Order's Emissary.

+20% vs Order

Phoenix Feather

Legendary

Auto-revive with 30% HP once per boss fight. Consumed on use. Restocks at rest points.

Auto-Revive (1/boss)

Consumables & Materials

Consumables provide temporary buffs or healing. Materials are used for weapon evolution and crafting. Both are found throughout the world or purchased from merchants.

Healing Salve

Restores 30% HP. 3-second use animation. Most common consumable.

Sha-Chi Crystal

Instantly grants 2 Sha-Chi bars. Rare drop, use strategically.

Sharpening Stone

+20% weapon damage for 30 seconds. Common in weapon chests.

Iron Ore

Basic evolution material. Found in all regions.

Phantom Essence

Rare evolution material (+3 and +4 upgrades). Boss drops.

Respec Tear

Refunds all skill points. Purchased from merchants after mid-game.
