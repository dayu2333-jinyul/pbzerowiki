👤 Character Profiles

Characters & NPCs

Profiles of every major character in Phantom Blade Zero — their backstory, role in the story, and connections to Soul's 66-day journey.

Home / Characters
⚔
Soul
Protagonist · Phantom Blade Assassin

The player character and an elite assassin of the Phantom Blade sect. Betrayed and poisoned, Soul has 66 days to find a cure and exact revenge. Master of Sha-Chi combat, his near-death experience has awakened latent powers even he didn't know he possessed. Soul's choices throughout the game determine which of the 8 endings the player receives.

Protagonist
⚡
The Healer
Mysterious Ally

A mysterious figure who saves Soul after his betrayal. The Healer belongs to a reclusive circle of Sha-Chi practitioners who use their abilities for preservation rather than combat. Their motives for saving Soul are unclear, and their knowledge of the curse suggests they know more than they reveal.

Ally Mysterious
💀
Sect Master Feng
Leader of the Phantom Blade Sect

The current leader of the Phantom Blade sect and Soul's mentor. A legendary warrior whose mastery of Sha-Chi is unmatched. Sect Master Feng's role in the conspiracy is ambiguous — he may have been involved in the betrayal, or he may be a victim of it himself.

Ambiguous
🔫
The Order's Emissary
Antagonist · Shadow Organization Operative

The primary human antagonist encountered throughout the game. The Emissary represents the Order's interests and appears at key story moments, often accompanied by elite guards. Their connection to Soul's betrayal is gradually revealed through documents and confrontations.

Antagonist
🧙
Iron Blade Chen
Fellow Phantom Blade Assassin

A fellow assassin of the Phantom Blade sect and Soul's former partner. Chen's loyalty is tested by the betrayal. He may become a valuable ally or a formidable enemy depending on player choices throughout the story.

Choice-Dependent
🎨
The Painter
Enigmatic NPC

A mysterious artist encountered in a remote temple. The Painter seems to know about the curse and Soul's fate. Their cryptic paintings contain clues about hidden locations, secret bosses, and the true nature of the Sha-Chi artifacts.

Mysterious
🛡
Seven Judgments
Boss · The Order's Enforcers

Not a single character but a group of three elite enforcers serving the Order. They are encountered as the Seven Judgments boss fight, each with distinct combat styles. Their defeat reveals crucial information about the Order's plans. See our Boss Strategies page for detailed fight guides.

Antagonist
🐉
Master Wei
Weapon Evolution Trainer

A reclusive weaponsmith who can evolve primary blades up to +4. Master Wei's knowledge of ancient forging techniques is essential for unlocking each weapon's full potential. He offers side quests that reward rare evolution materials.

Ally

Character Relationships

The relationships between characters in Phantom Blade Zero are shaped by player choices. Dialogue options, side quest completions, and the order in which you pursue story objectives all affect how characters perceive Soul. Some characters can become allies or enemies based on seemingly minor decisions.

💡 Relationship Tip:

Talk to every NPC after major story events. Their dialogue changes based on recent events, and some side quests are only available during specific time windows. Missing these can lock you out of certain endings.

For more on how character choices affect the game's conclusion, visit our Endings Guide. For a deeper look at the story's factions, check the Story & Lore page.
