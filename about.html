About PB Zero Wiki

Who we are, how we work, and why you can trust our Phantom Blade Zero guides and rankings.

👤 Who Runs This Wiki

PB Zero Wiki is maintained by a team of dedicated Phantom Blade Zero players who have spent hundreds of hours testing weapons, builds, and boss strategies. We are not affiliated with S-GAME — this is a community-driven, fan-made resource built to help players master every aspect of the game. Our editors include action RPG veterans with experience across the Souls-like and character-action genres who have been following PB Zero's development since its first reveal.

📊 Our Methodology

All rankings and tier lists follow a transparent scoring system with publicly stated criteria and weightings. We do not use "vibes-based" tiering. Every weapon, build, and boss difficulty rating is calculated from a weighted formula that we publish alongside each ranking:

  • Weapon Tier List: Damage (35%) + Speed (25%) + Sha-Chi Efficiency (25%) + Versatility (15%) — see full methodology →
  • Boss Difficulty: Attack Complexity (30%) + Damage Output (25%) + Phase Mechanics (25%) + Parry Difficulty (20%) — see full methodology →
  • Build Rankings: DPS Output (30%) + Survivability (25%) + Ease of Use (20%) + Boss Coverage (25%) — see full rankings →

📁 Data Sources

Our guides are built from multiple independent sources:

  • Official materials: S-GAME developer interviews, gameplay trailers, press demos, and official website information
  • Community testing: Data cross-referenced with r/PhantomBladeZero discussions, player footage analysis, and frame-data documentation
  • Action RPG benchmarks: Comparisons against Sekiro, Black Myth Wukong, and Nioh for context on difficulty and mechanics
  • Hands-on previews: Journalist and creator impressions from Gamescom and Tokyo Game Show

Where information is speculative (pre-release content), we clearly mark it with [Speculative] tags. Rankings are updated as new patch data and player testing become available.

🔍 Editorial Standards

  • Transparency: All rankings include publicly stated methodology. No hidden criteria or undisclosed sponsorships.
  • Correction policy: Errors reported by the community are investigated within 48 hours and corrected with a changelog entry.
  • No AI-generated rankings: All tier placements and difficulty scores are human-reviewed and playtested. We do not use LLM output for ranking decisions.
  • Version tracking: Every page includes a last-updated date. Post-release patches that rebalance weapons or bosses will trigger a full tier list review.

📬 Contact & Corrections

Found an error in a weapon stat, boss strategy, or ranking placement? Reach us through our contact page with the page URL and specific issue. Community contributions are credited in our changelog.
