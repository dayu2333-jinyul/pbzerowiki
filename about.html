👤 Who Runs This Wiki

honestly this whole thing started because I got obsessed with Phantom Blade Zero after that first gameplay trailer dropped and I couldn't find a single decent wiki that had all the weapon stats and boss strats in one place so I figured screw it I'll build one myself, I've sunk probably hundreds of hours into testing weapons and writing up boss guides and breaking down the Sha-Chi system frame by frame from preview footage, tbh this is a fan project run by someone who just really loves action RPGs and wanted a good resource that didn't feel like it was written by AI or copy-pasted from press releases, I've been playing Soulslikes and character action games since forever and been following PB Zero's development since day one, and nope I'm not affiliated with S-GAME at all this is completely community driven and I'm basically doing this because I think the game looks amazing and I want people to have a decent wiki when it launches

Our Methodology

so the way I do rankings is pretty simple honestly, everything has a public scoring formula with actual weights that I put right on each page so you can see exactly how I came up with the numbers, it's not like one of those tier lists where someone just goes "this feels S tier" based on vibes you know, every weapon and boss and build gets scored through a weighted formula that I publish alongside each ranking so you can check my math and stuff like that. For the weapon tier list I use Damage at 35% plus Speed at 25% plus Sha-Chi Efficiency at 25% plus Versatility at 15% and you can see the full breakdown on the tier list page, then for boss difficulty I break it down as Attack Complexity at 30% plus Damage Output at 25% plus Phase Mechanics at 25% plus Parry Difficulty at 20% and again there's a full methodology page for that, and for build rankings it's DPS Output at 30% plus Survivability at 25% plus Ease of Use at 20% plus Boss Coverage at 25% and all of this is documented with examples so you're never just taking my word for it

📁 Data Sources

I pull info from pretty much everywhere I can find it honestly, official stuff like S-GAME developer interviews and gameplay trailers and press demos and whatever they put on their website, but also I dig through Reddit threads on r/PhantomBladeZero and watch player footage frame by frame to try to figure out mechanics that haven't been officially confirmed yet, and I compare stuff against other action RPGs like Sekiro and Black Myth Wukong and Nioh to put the difficulty and mechanics in context because honestly if you've played those games you have a reference point for what to expect here. I also grab impressions from journalists and creators who got hands-on at Gamescom and Tokyo Game Show because they sometimes catch details that aren't in the official trailers. And any info that's speculative since the game isn't out yet gets clearly marked with Speculative tags so you know what's confirmed versus what's educated guessing, and I update rankings whenever new patch data or player testing comes out because I hate wikis that sit there outdated for months

Editorial Standards

I keep things pretty transparent around here, all rankings include publicly stated methodology and there's no hidden criteria or secret sponsorships or anything shady like that, if someone reports an error I investigate it within 48 hours and fix it with a changelog entry so you can see what changed and when, and I want to be really clear about this: no AI generates any of the rankings or tier placements, I personally review and playtest everything before it goes on the site because I've seen what happens when people just let LLMs spit out tier lists and it's usually garbage, every page includes a last-updated date and I'll do a full tier list review whenever post-release patches rebalance weapons or bosses because the meta is gonna shift hard once the game actually launches and people start optimizing properly

Contact & Corrections

if you spot something wrong with a weapon stat or a boss strategy or a ranking placement just hit up the contact page with the page URL and what specifically looks off, I try to fix stuff fast and I credit community contributions in the changelog because honestly you guys catch things I miss all the time and I appreciate it
