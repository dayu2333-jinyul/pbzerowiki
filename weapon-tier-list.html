How I Actually Score These Weapons

So each weapon gets an overall score based on a weighted formula I put together after way too many hours of testing and honestly it's not perfect but it gives you a pretty good idea of what to expect, Damage (35%) covers raw DPS output including special effects like bleed and fire procs and I've found that damage numbers can be misleading cause some weapons do great burst but awful sustained DPS

the other big one is Speed (25%) which is attack animation speed plus recovery frames and combo fluidity and tbh speed matters way more than people think cause a slow weapon with high damage is actually harder to use than a fast weapon with lower damage since you get punished way more for whiffing, then there's Sha-Chi Efficiency (25%) where lower bars equals more frequent power surge windows

honestly this stat alone can make or break a weapon because if you can't use your Power Surge often enough your overall DPS just tanks no matter how good the base numbers look, and finally Versatility (15%) which is basically how well the weapon performs across different bosses and mob types and builds and you'd be surprised how many weapons just fall apart against certain boss types and stuff like that

This methodology mirrors what the community has been voting on and I've cross-referenced it with actual gameplay testing data and I'll update rankings whenever new patch data comes out so if something gets buffed or nerfed you'll see it here first, well probably not first but you get the idea
