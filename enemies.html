Enemy Guide

A comprehensive bestiary of every enemy type in Phantom Blade Zero — regular foes, elite variants, and mini-bosses with attack patterns and weaknesses.

Home / Enemies

Regular Enemies

Standard enemies encountered throughout the world. While individually manageable, groups of regular enemies can overwhelm unprepared players. Knowing their attack patterns is key to efficient clearing.

Wasteland Bandit

Common

Basic human enemy with a rusty blade. Slow attacks with clear tells. Can be parried easily. Found in the Ruined Outskirts.

Weak to: Parry counters Ruined Outskirts

Wild Fang Beast

Common

Wolf-like creatures that attack in packs. Fast but low HP. Use wide-arc weapons to clear groups. Charge attack is a Brutal Move (blue flash).

Weak to: AoE attacks Ruined Outskirts

Sect Trainee

Common

Low-ranking Phantom Blade apprentices. Faster than bandits with more combo strings. Good practice for parry timing.

Weak to: Ghoststep Phantom Blade Fortress

Toxic Spitter

Common

Range enemy that fires poison projectiles. Priorities: close distance quickly. Their melee attack is weak and easily interrupted.

Weak to: Rushdown weapons Serpent's Maw Swamp

Serpent Soldier

Common

Snake-humanoid hybrids with spear attacks. Long reach but slow recovery. Punish after they miss a thrust.

Weak to: Parry after lunge Serpent's Maw Swamp

Order Sentinel

Common

Heavily armored Order soldiers with shields. Frontally protected — use Killer Moves or Ghoststep to attack from behind. Shield bash is a Brutal Move.

Weak to: Guard pierce weapons Order's Bastion

Shield Bearer

Common

Large shield-wielding elite infantry. Blocks frontal attacks. Use Ghost Razor (guard pierce) or flank with Ghoststep.

Weak to: Ghoststep behind Order's Bastion

Corrupted Monk

Common

Former healers twisted by dark Sha-Chi. Fast palm strikes and teleporting dashes. Unpredictable attack patterns.

Weak to: Slow heavy weapons Heaven's Reach Temple

Void Creature

Common

Reality-warped entities in the Abyss. Unpredictable attack patterns with teleportation. Boss-level stats in regular encounters.

Weak to: Elemental damage The Abyss

Elite Enemies

Elite variants are larger, tougher versions of regular enemies with expanded movesets. They often guard important items or shortcuts.

Bandit Chief

Elite

Oversized bandit with a greataxe. Slow but devastating. His charge attack covers surprising distance. Drops Iron Ore.

Ruined Outskirts

Sect Executioner

Elite

An elite Phantom Blade assassin sent to eliminate Soul. Dual-wields curved blades. Very aggressive — forces you to parry or die.

Phantom Blade Fortress

Order Commander

Elite

Elite Order officer with a greatsword. Wide sweep attacks and a ground slam that creates a shockwave. Mini-boss level HP.

Order's Bastion

Stone Guardian

Elite

Animated stone construct in Heaven's Reach Temple. Immune to bleed and poison. Slow but hits extremely hard. Use charged attacks to stagger.

Heaven's Reach Temple

Farming Tips

Material Farming

Best spot: Order's Bastion courtyard. 6 elite Order Sentinels respawn on rest. Each drops Iron Ore consistently.

XP Farming

Best spot: Serpent's Maw Swamp east path. Dense pack of 8 Serpent Soldiers + 2 Toxic Spitters in a corridor. Clear in 45 seconds.

Rare Drops

Phantom Essence (legendary material) drops from elite enemies on Hard+ difficulty. Farm Order Commanders in Bastion's throne room.
