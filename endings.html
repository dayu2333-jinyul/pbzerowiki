🎭 8 Endings

Endings Guide

Every ending in Phantom Blade Zero — how to unlock them, the key choices that matter, and what it takes to reach the true ending.

Home / Endings

Phantom Blade Zero features 8 distinct endings, branching from critical decisions made throughout the 66-day journey. Most endings are tied to Soul's relationships with key characters and factions, while the true ending requires extensive exploration and optional content completion.

True Ending — Redemption of the Blade

Canon: Likely Difficulty: Hard Requires: NG+ or perfect first run

Soul defeats the mastermind behind the conspiracy, breaks the 66-day curse, and restores balance to the Sha-Chi world. The Phantom Blade sect is reformed under new leadership. All loose ends are resolved — a complete, satisfying conclusion.

Requirements:
  • Collect all 8 Memory Fragments (hidden throughout every region)
  • Complete all major side quests
  • Save Iron Blade Chen (choice during Phantom Blade Fortress encounter)
  • Side with the Healer's Circle in the mid-game faction choice
  • Defeat the final secret boss before confronting the final boss

Revenge Ending — Blood for Blood

Difficulty: Easy Missable: No

Soul sacrifices everything to kill the mastermind, succumbing to the curse immediately after. A brutal but thematically consistent ending. Soul's dying act ensures the conspiracy dies with him.

Requirements: Default ending. Pursue the main story without engaging with side content or faction choices.

Order Ending — A New Order

Difficulty: Medium

Soul joins the Order, believing their control of Sha-Chi is necessary for world stability. The Order achieves its goal, but at the cost of Soul's freedom. Iron Blade Chen becomes your enemy.

Requirements: Side with the Order during the mid-game faction choice. Complete all Order-related quests.

Phantom Ending — Eternal Blade

Difficulty: Medium

Soul rejects both the Order and the Healer's Circle, choosing to remain with the Phantom Blade sect and rebuild it from within. The curse remains, but Soul has found purpose beyond survival.

Requirements: Stay loyal to the Phantom Blade sect throughout. Refuse all faction alliance offers. Iron Blade Chen must survive.

Sacrifice Ending — His Final Dawn

Difficulty: Medium

Soul uses his remaining Sha-Chi to save someone dear to him, expending the last of his life force. A tragic but noble end. Who you save depends on your relationships with other characters.

Requirements: Max relationship with a specific character (varies based on choices). Available only after completing their full questline.

Abyss Ending — Descent into Darkness

Difficulty: Hard

Soul embraces the dark Sha-Chi within, becoming a new threat worse than the Order. This ending is considered a "bad" ending but opens New Game+ with unique corrupted abilities.

Requirements: Collect dark Sha-Chi artifacts throughout the game (3 total). Choose dark-aligned dialogue options at critical story moments.

Wandering Ending — The Road Goes On

Difficulty: Easy

Soul walks away from everything — the sect, the curse, the conspiracy — choosing to spend his remaining days in peace. An ambiguous ending that leaves many questions unanswered.

Requirements: Refuse to engage with the main conflict. Walk away at three key decision points. Missable — requires specific dialogue choices.

Cycle Ending — 66 Days Again

Difficulty: Medium

Time resets. Soul wakes up at the beginning of the 66 days with all his memories intact. A New Game+ setup ending that implies​ Soul's struggle is a repeating cycle.

Requirements: Defeat the final boss without resolving the curse. Must have failed to collect at least 3 Memory Fragments.
Ending Note: The "Redemption of the Blade" is widely considered the canonical ending based on S-GAME's narrative direction. If you want one complete playthrough, aim for this ending. The other 7 endings are best experienced on subsequent playthroughs using New Game+, which carries over weapon upgrades and skill points.
