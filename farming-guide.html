Best Farming Spots in Phantom Blade Zero

Where to grind spirit coins, evolution materials, and rare drops at every stage of the game. Tested routes with per-hour estimates so you know exactly what you're getting into.

Home / Farming Guide

I'm going to be real with you: farming in Phantom Blade Zero isn't bad. The respawn system is generous, bell checkpoints are everywhere, and drop rates for common materials are reasonable. You'll mostly farm because you want to try a new weapon and need to catch it up to your main, not because the game forces you to grind.

That said, if you're going to farm, do it efficiently. Here are the best spots, ranked by what you're actually getting per minute of your time.

S Tier

Bloodsoaked Arena — Endgame Coin Farm

Zone 7 · Final Dungeon Entrance

This is the best farming spot in the game, period. Right before the final boss fog gate, there's an arena where three elite enemies spawn in sequence. They respawn every time you rest at the nearby bell — no loading screens, no long runbacks.

  • Route: Rest at the Final Approach bell → kill three Crimson Phantoms in the arena → rest again. Each clear takes about 45 seconds with a maxed weapon.
  • Drops: 2,000-3,000 coins per clear, plus a roughly 15% chance at a +3 evolution stone from each Phantom. Occasionally drops +2 stones as well.
  • Tip: Equip the Greedy Pendant accessory (10% bonus coins) from the Crossroads Bazaar merchant. It pays for itself in about 20 minutes of farming here.
A Tier

Serpent Cultist Path — Midgame Material Farm

Zone 2 · Whispering Grotto Depths

The best midgame spot for sword and spear evolution materials. The path between the Grotto Depths bell and the Serpent Cultist boss arena has six cultists packed into a straight line.

  • Enemy types: Four Serpent Cultists (sword evolution stones), two Spear Acolytes (spear evolution stones). The Acolytes drop +1 spear stones at about a 20% rate.
  • Bonus: The Serpent Cultist mini-boss at the end of this path respawns and drops +2 sword stones at roughly 25%. Worth killing him on each loop if you have the damage output.
  • When to use: Once your weapon hits +1 and you're struggling with the Zone 3 boss. Three loops here will get you to +2.
A Tier

Collapsed Mineshaft — Greatsword & Dual Blade Stones

Zone 4 · Eastern Mining Tunnels

If you're running a greatsword or dual blade build, this is your spot. The mining tunnels are filled with Miner Phantoms that drop both greatsword and dual blade evolution stones.

  • Route: Start at the Tunnel Entrance bell → clear the three mining chambers in sequence → loop back. Each chamber has 4-5 enemies.
  • Watch out: The third chamber has a Rockbreaker elite that hits like a truck. Kill the small enemies first, then fight the elite one-on-one.
  • Drop rates: +1 greatsword stones at ~18%, +1 dual blade stones at ~15%. Occasional +2 stones from the Rockbreaker.
B Tier

Garrison Courtyard — Early Game Starter Farm

Zone 1 · Abandoned Garrison

Not the most efficient farm, but it's the earliest one available. The courtyard between the Garrison Entrance bell and the Commander boss arena has seven sword soldiers that respawn quickly.

  • Coins: About 3,000 per hour. Bad by later standards, but useful when you're saving for the Thunderclap Dual Blades at the Bazaar.
  • Combat practice: This is actually a better parry training ground than a farm. The soldiers have predictable blue-flash attacks, and you can practice parry timing here for 20 minutes and come out significantly better at the game.
  • Only farm here if: You're stuck on the first boss and need a few more levels or want the merchant weapons. Otherwise, move on — better spots open up fast.

Farming Tips

  • Greedy Pendant is worth it. Buy it as soon as you reach the Crossroads Bazaar. The +10% coin bonus applies to everything and the accessory slot competition isn't fierce in the early game.
  • Don't farm before Zone 2. The Garrison farm is inefficient. Push to the Whispering Grotto and farm there if you really need materials.
  • Bell resting resets enemies. Unlike some soulslikes where enemies only respawn on death or zone transition, resting at a bell in PB Zero fully resets the area. This makes farming loops fast.
  • Phantom Edge drops are not farmable. Enemy-dropped Phantom Edges are first-kill guaranteed and then never drop again. Don't waste time trying to farm duplicates.
