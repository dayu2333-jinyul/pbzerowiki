Weapons Guide

A deep get into every weapon type in Phantom Blade Zero — combat styles, Sha-Chi costs, and recommended combos for every playstyle.

Home / Weapons / Weapons Guide

Overview

Phantom Blade Zero ships with 30+ primary blades and 20+ Phantom Edges at launch. You can carry two primary weapons and two Phantom Edges in your loadout at any time. Each primary blade has its own upgrade tree with 4 evolution levels, unlocking new combo strings, passive bonuses, and enhanced Power Surge abilities. Weapon choice defines your combat rhythm — picking the right setup for your playstyle is the single most impactful decision you will make.

Sword

Sword

Balanced damage and speed. Jack of all trades, master of parrying.

Swords sit in the middle of the damage/speed spectrum. They have the most generous parry windows in the game, making them the go-to choice for counter-focused players. The Venomous Softblade extends parry frames further and drains enemy Sha-Chi on successful parries. Jagged Steel trades some parry utility for bleed buildup, ideal for sustained pressure. Flame Tongue combusts bleed stacks into fire explosions for extra burst.

Recommended Combo: Lead with 3 light attacks to build bleed stacks, then follow with a charged heavy to trigger combustion. Use parry to reset enemy aggression and repeat.

Best paired with: Blood Sigil (Phantom Edge) for sustain, or Chain Hook to close distance.

Ideal build: Defensive Parry Master. Sword's extended parry windows reward counter-play over aggression.

Greatsword

Greatsword

Maximum damage per hit. Slow but devastating.

Greatswords hit harder than any other weapon type. Savage Axe has absurd stagger damage and breaks enemy guard in 2-3 hits. Ironbreaker reduces enemy block efficiency with each landed blow. Thunder Edge releases AoE electric bursts on charged attacks. Earthshaker is the heaviest hitter in the game — 100 damage rating and massive stun potential, but its speed rating of 20 means you commit hard to every swing.

Recommended Combo: Open with a charged heavy to break guard, then immediately Power Surge for full damage. Against mobile enemies, bait an attack, Ghoststep behind them, then unleash a fully charged heavy.

Best paired with: Dragon Fire (Arm Cannon) to poke during your own recovery frames, or Gale Fan for knockback.

Ideal build: Aggressive Chi Rush — greatswords need maximum Sha-Chi generation to sustain their high-cost Power Surges.

Spear

Spear

Excellent reach and crowd control. Keep enemies at the tip of your blade.

Spears excel at controlling space. Twilight Spear has sweeping attacks that hit multiple enemies, making it the best weapon for group fights. Shadow Stinger applies poison buildup with quick thrusts, slowing enemies over time. Storm Piercer fires a piercing gust through enemies in a line — devastating in corridors. Celestial Lance deals bonus holy damage and its Power Surge summons a beam from above for massive single-target damage.

Recommended Combo: Sweeping light attacks to control spacing, then forward+heavy for a lunging thrust. Against groups, use charged heavy for the wide arc sweep, then follow with a Phantom Edge AoE.

Best paired with: Void Rift (Phantom Edge) to group enemies into your spear sweeps, or Ice Dart to slow dangerous targets.

Ideal build: Hybrid Flex — spear's versatility lets you adapt to any situation without sacrificing effectiveness.

Dual Blades

Dual Blades

The fastest weapon class. Rapid hits build Sha-Chi faster than anything else.

Dual blades sacrifice individual hit damage for the fastest attack speed in the game. White Python & Red Viper are the signature dual blades — alternating strikes build Sha-Chi quickly and apply stacking damage. Crescent Moon has spinning slash attacks with wide arc coverage. Phoenix Wing triggers fire explosions on every third consecutive hit. Blood Thorn has the lowest damage but highest speed (98 rating) and lifesteal on every hit, making it the best sustain weapon in the game.

Recommended Combo: Full light attack string to max out Sha-Chi, then spend it all on Power Surge. Against bosses, weave in dodges between light attacks to maintain pressure without taking damage.

Best paired with: Spirit Wolf (Phantom Edge) to create distractions while you build combos, or Thunderbolt Shard for AoE.

Ideal build: Aggressive Chi Rush — dual blades generate Sha-Chi faster than any other weapon, maximizing Power Surge uptime.

Arm Cannon

Arm Cannon

Ranged pressure with high burst damage. High Sha-Chi cost, high payoff.

Arm Cannons are ranged primary weapons that consume Sha-Chi to fire. Dragon Fire fires charged energy shots that pierce through enemies. Demon Arm drains Sha-Chi from enemies on hit, creating a self-sustaining loop. Arm Cannons have limited ammo that regenerates over time, so you cannot rely on them as your sole weapon — they are best used to apply pressure from range before closing in with your primary blade.

Recommended Combo: Open with 1-2 charged shots to force the enemy to close distance, then parry their approach attack and switch to melee. Use Arm Cannon during enemy recovery windows for safe damage.

Best paired with: Any Greatsword — soften enemies from range, then finish with heavy melee blows.

Ideal build: Hybrid Flex or Aggressive Chi Rush with ranged focus.

Phantom Edges (Secondary Weapons)

Phantom Edges

20+ utility tools that consume 1-2 Sha-Chi bars. Cooldown: 8-15 seconds.

Phantom Edges are secondary weapons that complement your primary blade. They cost Sha-Chi to use and have individual cooldowns. They cannot be upgraded, but their effects scale with your primary weapon's evolution level. Phantom Edges cover six categories: ranged damage, crowd control, sustain, mobility, debuffs, and summons.

Weapon Evolution System

Every primary blade has 4 evolution levels. Each level unlocks new abilities:

  • +1 Evolution: New basic combo string
  • +2 Evolution: Passive stat bonus (crit rate, damage, or speed)
  • +3 Evolution: Enhanced Power Surge ability
  • +4 Evolution (Max): Unique weapon mastery skill
Key Tip: Evolution materials are found in specific regions. Before committing to a weapon, check whether you can farm its required materials at your current progress level. Some +3 and +4 materials are locked behind late-game bosses.
