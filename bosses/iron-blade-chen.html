Iron Blade Chen — Boss Guide

Iron Blade Chen is an optional boss found in the Phantom Blade Trainee training grounds. He's not required to complete the game, but beating him drops the Venomous Softblade, which is the best weapon in the game for Parry Master builds. The fight is a mirror match in the worst way. Chen uses the same Phantom Blade techniques you do. He parries, he Ghoststeps, and in phase two he starts reading your inputs. If you've been coasting on aggression without learning the defensive mechanics, Chen is the wake-up call.

The arena is a circular training courtyard surrounded by Phantom Blade Trainees who watch but don't interfere. The enclosed space means you can't create distance easily. Chen controls the center of the arena and you're fighting from the edges. This matters because his phase-two gap-closer covers the entire arena in about half a second.

Phase One: The Student

Chen opens the fight with basic sword combos. Three-hit strings with a slight pause between the second and third hit. The third hit is a Brutal Move (blue flash) every time. Parry it. This is the game's way of testing whether you've learned the parry mechanic before the real fight starts. If you're blocking instead of parrying, you'll run out of Sha-Chi within the first minute and die.

After each parry, Chen staggers for about two seconds. Land 2-3 hits, then back off. He has a counter-parry move that triggers if you're still attacking when the stagger ends. The tell for the counter is a brief sword-spin animation. If you see him spin the blade, dodge backward immediately. The counter-parry is a Killer Move (red flash) with almost no wind-up. It's one of the fastest red flashes in the game and it catches aggressive players constantly. Dodge into it for a Ghoststep, but honestly, it's easier to just not trigger the counter in the first place. Two hits, back off, wait for the next combo.

At 70% health, Chen starts mixing in a thrust attack that covers about half the arena. The thrust is a Brutal Move with a longer tell than his combo finisher. The wind-up has him pull the sword back to his hip before lunging. Parry timing is slightly later than the combo finisher. Wait for the forward movement to start, then parry. Too early and you'll whiff, leaving you open to the follow-up slash.

Phase Two: The Master

At 40% health, Chen drops the student act. His sword glows with Sha-Chi and his attack patterns change completely. He gains three new moves that define the second half of the fight. First: a dash-cross slash that covers the entire arena. It's a Killer Move, so you can't block it. Dodge into it for a Ghoststep. The timing is generous compared to his counter-parry. Wait for the dash sound effect, count one beat, dodge. If you Ghoststep this move consistently, phase two becomes manageable.

Second: a Sha-Chi wave projectile. Chen swings his sword in a wide arc and sends a crescent of energy across the arena. It's a Brutal Move that you can parry, and parrying it reflects the wave back at Chen for significant damage. The timing is the same as parrying his combo finisher in phase one. The wave travels fast but the tell is huge. He always sheathes the sword briefly before the wave. Sheath, pause, swing. Parry on the swing.

Third: a healing stance. Chen steps back, plants his sword in the ground, and regenerates health. This is the fight's DPS check. If you don't deal enough damage during the stance to exceed his healing, the fight extends indefinitely. Use your heaviest attacks the moment you see him plant the sword. The Iron Tide build trivializes this phase because the Savage Axe charged heavy out-damages his healing by a wide margin. With Parry Master, you'll need to land the full post-Ghoststep combo plus a Phantom Edge ability to break the heal. With Hybrid Flex, Dragon Fire charged shots from range will do it if you start shooting immediately.

Recommended Builds and Strategy

Parry Master is the safest choice for a first clear. The extended parry windows make phase one consistent and the Bow of Silent Wind lets you poke safely while learning phase two's patterns. Venomous Softblade's extended parry frames are the entire reason to run this build against Chen. Landing the counter-parry dodge on his Killer Moves is harder than it looks, so having more margin on the Brutal Moves keeps your Sha-Chi healthy.

Iron Tide can kill Chen faster but the counter-parry in phase one will end runs if you're too greedy. The rule is simple: two hits, back off, no exceptions. If you follow that rule, Iron Tide's damage output overwhelms his healing stance without needing a special strategy. If you get impatient and go for a third hit, you will eat the counter-parry and probably die.

Drop: Venomous Softblade (guaranteed first kill). This weapon has the longest parry frames in the game and is essentially required for high-difficulty Parry Master play. Chen also drops 3-5 skill points and a rare evolution material for swords. Worth farming on higher difficulties for the material drops even after you have the blade.
