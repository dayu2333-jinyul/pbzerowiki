Primary Blades

So primary blades are your main weapon slot and they define pretty much everything about how you play, your basic attack strings and special moves and Power Surge abilities all come from whatever blade you have equipped and honestly each weapon type feels like a completely different game which is kind of insane, like playing with a Greatsword versus Dual Blades isn't just a stat difference it's a whole different approach to combat

some bosses that absolutely destroy you with one weapon type become almost trivial with another

Phantom Edges Overview

So Phantom Edges are basically your secondary weapons and they consume Sha-Chi bars to use

I honestly slept on these for my entire first playthrough which was a huge mistake because some of them are absolutely game-changing once you learn how to use them right, they can't be upgraded which kind of sucks but they do get bonus effects based on your equipped primary blade's evolution level so the higher your main weapon is evolved the more powerful your Edges become and that synergy is honestly the difference between struggling through boss fights

just cruising through them

20+ Phantom Edges confirmed in the game so far and I've unlocked most of them and tbh some are way better than others, you've got ranged bows throwing daggers chain hooks and all these weird magical off-hand tools and stuff like that, each one costs 1-2 Sha-Chi bars to activate and has a cooldown of about 8-15 seconds and the cooldown management is pretty much the whole game with these things
Shadow Chakram
Boomerang projectile, returns on hit. 2 bars.
Chain Hook
Pulls enemies toward you. 1 bar.
Thunderbolt Shard
Lightning AoE around player. 2 bars.
Mist Step
Short teleport, no i-frames. 1 bar.
Gale Fan
Knocks back surrounding enemies. 2 bars.
Blood Sigil
Heals 15% HP on next hit. 2 bars.
Ice Dart
Fires freezing projectile that slows one enemy. 1 bar.
Flame Vial
Throws a fire bomb dealing AoE damage over time. 2 bars.
Spirit Wolf
Summons a spectral wolf that attacks twice then fades. 2 bars.
Void Rift
Creates a black hole that pulls enemies together. 2 bars.
Steel Thread
Immobilizes an enemy for 3 seconds. 1 bar.
Poison Mist
Creates a toxic cloud that applies poison to all inside. 2 bars.

Weapon Evolution System

So each primary blade can be evolved through upgrade trees using materials you find scattered around the world and this system is honestly way deeper than it looks at first, you start at +1 which unlocks a new basic combo string and already the weapon feels different and more fluid and then +2 adds a passive stat bonus like crit rate or damage or speed

this is where builds really start coming together because the right stat bonus can turn a decent weapon into an absolute monster, +3 unlocks an enhanced Power Surge ability and honestly this is where the game changes completely because evolved Power Surges hit like a truck and I've had moments where a single evolved Surge just deleted 60% of a boss's health bar and I literally sat there with my jaw on the floor,

finally +4 which is the max gives you a unique weapon mastery skill and tbh you haven't really experienced a weapon until you've taken it all the way to +4 because the transformation is kind of ridiculous and some weapons that feel terrible at +0 become your favorite weapon at +4 so don't write anything off until you've fully evolved it and stuff like that
