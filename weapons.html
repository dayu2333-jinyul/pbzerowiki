Phantom Edges Overview

Phantom Edges are secondary weapons that consume Sha-Chi bars to use. They cannot be upgraded but gain bonus effects based on your equipped primary blade's evolution level.

20+ Phantom Edges confirmed in the game, including ranged bows, throwing daggers, chain hooks, and magical off-hand tools. Each Phantom Edge costs 1-2 Sha-Chi bars to activate and has a cooldown of 8-15 seconds.

Weapon Evolution System

Each primary blade can be evolved through upgrade trees using materials found in the world. Evolution unlocks new skills and passive effects:

  • +1 Evolution: Unlocks a new basic combo string
  • +2 Evolution: Adds a passive stat bonus (crit rate, damage, or speed)
  • +3 Evolution: Unlocks an enhanced Power Surge ability
  • +4 Evolution (Max): Unlocks unique weapon mastery skill
