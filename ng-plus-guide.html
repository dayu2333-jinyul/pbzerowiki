🔄 New Game Plus

Phantom Blade Zero New Game Plus Guide

What carries over, what changes, and what you should absolutely finish before starting your second run. Plus: the NG+ exclusive weapons and bosses most players miss.

Home / New Game Plus Guide

If you're coming from a Souls game, you probably expect NG+ to just be the same thing with bigger health bars. Phantom Blade Zero does it differently — NG+ remixes enemy placements, adds entirely new attack strings to bosses, and unlocks weapons that don't exist in a first playthrough. It's closer to what Nioh does with its NG+ cycles than what Dark Souls does.

What Carries Over to NG+

Here's the complete checklist. Some things carry over that might surprise you, and some things don't that really should.

✓
All weapons and their evolution levels
Every primary weapon and Phantom Edge you collected, at exactly the upgrade level you left them. This is the biggest reason to farm rare materials before NG+.
✓
All accessories and equipment
Rings, pendants, armor pieces — everything in your inventory stays.
✓
Spirit coins (currency)
Full carryover. If you're sitting on 50k coins at the end of your first run, you start NG+ rich.
✓
Unlocked skills (partial)
Combat skills carry over. Story-gated abilities (like Dual Phantom Edge) must be re-unlocked through story progression.
✓
Bell checkpoint unlocks
You keep fast travel to every bell you rang. This makes NG+ much faster for routing.
✗
NPC relationship progress
All affinity resets to zero. If you're aiming for a specific ending, you'll need to rebuild those relationships.
✗
Key items and quest items
Story keys, quest flags, and progression items are wiped. You'll replay quests from scratch.
✗
Faction reputation
Resets completely. Faction choices in NG+ are independent of your first playthrough.

What Changes in NG+

NG+ isn't just "the same but harder." S-GAME actually put work into making the second run feel different:

  • Enemy damage scales to +40% across the board. Health pools go up about 25%. It's not a linear multiplier — early-game enemies get a bigger boost than late-game enemies, so the difficulty curve flattens out.
  • Bosses get new attack strings. Every major boss has 1-3 new moves that only appear in NG+. The Commander of the Iron Steed, for example, gets a delayed follow-up to his charge attack that punishes the dodge timing you drilled into muscle memory on your first run.
  • Enemy placements shuffle. Some early zones have late-game enemies mixed in. The Abandoned Garrison now has a Crimson Phantom patrolling the courtyard — an enemy type you didn't see until Zone 4 on a first playthrough.
  • New Phantom Edge drops. Certain enemies drop Phantom Edge variants in NG+ that simply don't exist in the base game's loot table.

NG+ Exclusive Weapons

There are weapons you can only get in New Game Plus. Here are the confirmed ones:

  • Soulrender (Sword): Drops from the final boss on NG+. Has a unique passive that converts 10% of damage dealt into Sha-Chi. Arguably the best sword in the game.
  • Ashen Reaper (Greatsword): Found in the prologue area on NG+ — replaces the Jagged Steel pickup. Faster swing speed than any other greatsword, with only slightly reduced damage.
  • Twin Fangs (Dual Blades): Reward for completing the NG+ version of Yuelin's quest chain (she has an extended quest in NG+ that reveals more of her backstory).

Before You Start NG+ — Completion Checklist

Some things are much harder or impossible to do after you reset. Take care of these first:

  • Farm rare evolution materials. The final boss arena has respawning elite enemies that drop +3 evolution stones. Farm them until your main weapons are maxed — those enemies are replaced in NG+ with much tougher versions.
  • Finish all NPC quest chains. Even if you're going for a different ending on NG+, complete every available quest first. Some NPCs give accessories that carry over, and the quest knowledge will save you time on your next run.
  • Collect all Phantom Edges. The achievement for full Phantom Edge collection requires base-game versions. NG+ exclusive variants don't count toward it.
  • Unlock all bell checkpoints. Fast travel points carry over, which makes NG+ routing dramatically faster. Spend 30 minutes running through every zone and ringing every bell you missed.
  • Buy out merchant inventories. Coins carry over but merchant stock resets. Spend your extra currency on consumables and upgrade materials before restarting.

NG+ Ending Routes

NG+ is your chance to see the endings you missed. Since NPC relationships reset, you have a clean slate for faction choices. The most common approach: use your first playthrough for the "natural" ending (whatever your choices lead to), then target the True Ending on NG+ using a guide. The True Ending requires specific relationship thresholds with three NPCs and two faction decisions that are easy to miss without planning ahead.
