🔍 Exploration Guide

Hidden Secrets & Easter Eggs in Phantom Blade Zero

S-GAME stuffed this game with hidden stuff. Breakable walls that look like regular walls, invisible bridges over bottomless pits, NPCs who only appear at specific times, and at least two secret bosses nobody found in the demo. Here's everything worth finding, organized by zone.

Home / Hidden Secrets

A quick note before we start: Phantom Blade Zero is the kind of game where the developers hide real content behind secrets — not just collectibles or lore notes, but actual weapons, Phantom Edges, and boss fights. If you're rushing through the main path, you're probably missing 30% of the game.

I've organized these by zone to avoid spoiling later areas. Everything here is from the first half of the game. No ending spoilers.

🧱

Cracked Wall in the Prologue Training Grounds

Prologue · Training Grounds

After you pick up your first weapon and the game teaches you the light attack combo, there's a small alcove on the right side of the courtyard. The wall at the back of the alcove has faint crack lines — they're easy to miss because the lighting in this area is dim. Hit it with any heavy attack and it breaks open.

  • Reward: Blood Sigil Phantom Edge. This is the earliest heal Phantom Edge in the game, and you can have it before your first real fight.
  • Why it matters: Having a heal before the first boss changes the entire early game. Most players don't find Blood Sigil until much later.
🌉

Invisible Bridge — Abandoned Garrison Rooftops

Zone 1 · Abandoned Garrison

On the highest rooftop of the Garrison — the one you reach by climbing the collapsed watchtower — if you look toward the distant mountain peak, there's a gap between two buildings that looks like a death drop. It's not. Walk off the edge toward the smaller rooftop and you'll land on an invisible platform.

  • How to find it: Stand at the very edge of the highest rooftop and throw a pebble (any consumable works). If the pebble stops mid-air, there's a platform.
  • Crossing: The invisible path zigzags twice. Walk slowly — the platforms aren't huge.
  • Reward: Thunderbolt Shard Phantom Edge (ranged lightning attack) and a +1 sword evolution stone.
👻

The Mourning NPC — Whispering Grotto

Zone 2 · Whispering Grotto Missable

After defeating the Serpent Cultist mini-boss, backtrack to the grotto entrance before ringing the next bell checkpoint. There's an NPC kneeling by the water who wasn't there before. Her name is Yuelin, and she has a short quest chain that spans three zones.

  • Quest reward: Moonlit Pendant accessory — increases Sha-Chi generation by 15%. One of the best accessories in the game and completely missable.
  • Trigger condition: You must talk to her before ringing the bell in the grotto depths. Ring that bell and she disappears permanently for this playthrough.
  • Follow-up: She reappears in the Crossroads Bazaar after you complete her first task. The quest chain is three steps total.
🪨

False Wall Behind the Waterfall — Crystal Caverns

Zone 3 · Crystal Caverns

In the large underground lake area of the Crystal Caverns, there's a waterfall on the far left wall. Walk through it — there's a hidden chamber behind it. This one is actually telegraphed if you pay attention: there are glowing blue crystals on either side of the waterfall that don't appear anywhere else in the cavern.

  • Inside: A single elite enemy (Crystal Guardian) guarding a chest.
  • Reward: Frostbite Edge Phantom Edge (ice damage, slows enemies) and 2,500 spirit coins.
  • Note: The Crystal Guardian is tougher than the zone boss. Come prepared or come back later.
🔔

The Silent Bell — Forgotten Temple Basement

Zone 3 · Forgotten Temple Missable

In the temple basement, past the rows of kneeling statues, there's a bell checkpoint that doesn't ring. It's covered in rust and the prompt says "Ring Bell" but nothing happens. Most players shrug and move on. Don't.

  • Solution: Use the Blood Sigil Phantom Edge while standing next to the bell. The blood offering cleanses the rust and the bell rings, unlocking a hidden boss arena.
  • Secret boss: Fallen Bellkeeper — a remixed version of the tutorial boss with new attack patterns. Drops the Bellkeeper's Oath accessory (+20% Sha-Chi from parries).
  • Hint from the devs: An NPC in the Crossroads Bazaar mentions "a bell that thirsts" if you talk to him three times. That's your clue.
🗡️

Developer Room — Crossroads Bazaar

Zone 2 · Crossroads Bazaar

Behind the weapon merchant's stall, there's a narrow alley you can squeeze through. At the end of the alley is a wooden door with no prompt. Attack it 66 times (yes, really — it's a reference to Soul's 66-day countdown) and it breaks open.

  • Inside: A small room with developer messages on the walls, concept art you can view, and a chest containing 6,666 spirit coins.
  • Easter egg: One of the wall messages reads "Sorry about the greatsword tracking" — a self-aware nod from S-GAME about a known issue in the demo build.

Tips for Finding Your Own Secrets

S-GAME uses consistent environmental language to hint at secrets. Once you learn their visual language, you'll spot them everywhere:

  • Cracked surfaces — always breakable. Look for hairline fractures on walls, floors, and even ceilings.
  • Isolated glowing objects — if a single torch, crystal, or lantern is lit in an otherwise dark area, it's marking something.
  • NPC repetition — any NPC who says something cryptic three times is giving you a clue. Talk to everyone multiple times.
  • Out-of-place silence — areas where the background music suddenly cuts out usually have something hidden.
