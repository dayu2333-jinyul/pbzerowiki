About Phantom Blade Zero

Phantom Blade Zero is an action RPG by S-GAME, releasing September 9, 2026 on PS5 and PC. You play as Soul, an elite assassin betrayed and resurrected with only 66 days to live. The game features a unique Sha-Chi combat system blending aggressive offense with precise parry mechanics, 30+ weapons, semi-open world exploration, and 8 distinct endings.

PB Zero FAQ — Frequently Asked Questions

When does Phantom Blade Zero release?
September 9, 2026 on PS5 and PC (Steam). Pre-orders expected to open mid-2026.
Is Phantom Blade Zero harder than Sekiro?
It's different — PB Zero rewards aggressive offense with the Sha-Chi system. No stamina bar, fast-paced combat. Some bosses are tougher, but the combat system gives you more tools.
How many weapons are in Phantom Blade Zero?
30+ primary weapons and 20+ Phantom Edges (secondary weapons). Each weapon has unique move sets, combo strings, and upgrade paths.
How many endings does PB Zero have?
8 distinct endings. Your choices, NPC relationships, and boss outcomes determine which ending you unlock.
What platforms is PB Zero on?
PlayStation 5 and PC (Steam). An Xbox Series X|S or Switch 2 release has not been announced.
