About Phantom Blade Zero

Phantom Blade Zero is an action RPG by S-GAME releasing September 9, 2026 on PS5 and PC. You play as Soul, an elite assassin betrayed and left for dead, resurrected with only 66 days to live.

The combat is built around Sha-Chi — no stamina bar, just pure aggression mixed with precise parry timing. There are 30+ weapons, a semi-open world, and 8 different endings depending on your choices and NPC relationships.

I've been following this game since the first trailer and honestly, the combat feels so fluid once you get the rhythm down it's kinda ruined other action games for me.

PB Zero FAQ, Frequently Asked Questions

When does Phantom Blade Zero release?
September 9, 2026 on PS5 and PC (Steam). Pre-orders expected to open mid-2026. No collector's edition announced yet.
Is Phantom Blade Zero harder than Sekiro?
Different kind of difficulty. PB Zero rewards aggression — no stamina bar, so you can keep swinging. Some bosses are definitely tougher, but the Sha-Chi system gives you more defensive tools than Sekiro. About the same overall, just in a different way.
How many weapons are in Phantom Blade Zero?
30+ primary weapons (blades) and 20+ Phantom Edges (secondary weapons like cannons, lances, axes). Each has unique movesets, combo strings, and upgrade paths.
How many endings does PB Zero have?
8 distinct endings. Which one you get depends on NPC side quests, which bosses you spare or kill, and key story decisions. The true ending requires very specific conditions.
What platforms is PB Zero on?
PlayStation 5 and PC (Steam). Xbox Series X|S and Switch 2 haven't been announced. Day one on both PS5 and PC.
