Release Date Confirmed: September 9, 2026

S-GAME has officially announced September 9, 2026 as the global release date for Phantom Blade Zero. The game will launch simultaneously on PlayStation 5 and PC (Steam). Pre-orders are expected to open in the summer months.

Gameplay Showcase at Summer Game Fest 2025

Phantom Blade Zero was featured at Summer Game Fest 2025 with an extended gameplay demonstration. The showcase revealed the Seven Judgments boss fight, the Serpent's Maw Swamp region, and the first look at the Arm Cannon weapon type.

Year of the Snake Trailer Debuts New Boss

To celebrate Lunar New Year 2025, S-GAME released the "Year of the Snake" trailer featuring the Snake Sovereign boss fight. The trailer showcased the serpentine boss's whip-like attacks and the Serpent's Maw Swamp environment.

February 2026
Interview

S-GAME Interview: Combat Design Philosophy

In a developer interview, S-GAME's creative director discussed the Sha-Chi combat system design philosophy. Key takeaways: the team studied Sekiro's posture system and Chinese martial arts cinema, aiming to create a combat system that rewards aggression without punishing mistakes too harshly. The dual-type Brutal/Killer Move system was born from wanting to add decision-making to reactive combat.

January 2026
Trailer

ChinaJoy 2025 Demo Opens for Public

Phantom Blade Zero had a playable demo at ChinaJoy 2025, giving fans the first hands-on experience. The demo featured the opening area (Ruined Outskirts), the Ogre Hammer Lord boss fight, and three weapons to test. Early impressions praised the responsive combat and visual quality.

November 2025
Trailer

Phantom Blade Zero Official Reveal Trailer

The first official trailer for Phantom Blade Zero was released at Gamescom 2023, revealing the game's dark fantasy setting, the Sha-Chi combat system, and the protagonist Soul. The trailer received widespread attention for its stylish action sequences and UE5 visuals.
